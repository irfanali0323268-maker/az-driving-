Driving Lessons FAQs

Quick answers to common questions about lessons, prices, booking and tests.

General Driving Lessons

Where do you teach?

Yes. We offer lessons in Gloucester, Stroud, Tewkesbury and Cheltenham.

How many lessons will I need?

It varies for each learner. Your instructor will assess your progress and advise honestly.

Do you teach complete beginners?

Yes. Our beginner lessons start from lesson one, at your own pace.

I have not driven for a while. Can you help?

Yes. Refresher lessons are designed to rebuild your confidence after a break.

Manual & Automatic Lessons

Do you offer both manual and automatic lessons?

Yes, manual and automatic lessons are available.

Should I learn in a manual or an automatic car?

An automatic car has fewer controls, so you can concentrate on the road. A manual licence lets you drive both types of car. Your instructor can talk through which suits you best.

Driving Theory Tests

What is in the car theory test?

50 multiple-choice questions in 57 minutes (pass mark 43), then hazard perception: 14 clips (pass mark 44 out of 75).

How can I prepare for my UK driving theory test?

Study the Highway Code, practise multiple-choice and hazard perception questions, then book through GOV.UK. Our theory test page has a practice quiz.

Practical Driving Tests

How do I book a practical test?

Practical tests are booked through GOV.UK. Your instructor can advise when you are ready.
