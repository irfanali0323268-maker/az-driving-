Theory Test Tips: How to Pass First Time

By AZ Driving School · 12 November 2026 · 5 min read

The theory test is the first big hurdle on the way to your licence, and it is very passable with a bit of steady preparation. Here is what it involves and how to get ready.

The format

The car theory test has two parts, taken in one sitting:

  • Multiple choice: 50 questions in 57 minutes. You need 43 out of 50 to pass. It includes a short case study with five questions.
  • Hazard perception: 14 video clips with a pass mark of 44 out of 75.

You must pass both parts in the same sitting. Your pass is valid for two years, so you need to pass your practical test within that time.

Tips that work

  1. Read the Highway Code. It is free on GOV.UK and the questions are based on it. Little and often beats a last-minute cram.
  2. Practise daily. Ten or fifteen minutes a day helps answers stick. Try our free practice quiz and use the official practice resources on GOV.UK.
  3. Learn the road signs by shape and colour. Triangles warn, circles give orders, rectangles inform.
  4. Read each question twice. Many marks are lost to rushing, and some questions ask what you should not do.
  5. Use your lessons. Noticing real hazards from the passenger seat or behind the wheel helps with hazard perception.
  6. Practise the hazard clips. You score for spotting a developing hazard early, so click when you see it start to develop. Do not click constantly, as that can score zero for the clip.
  7. Take a mock test. Time yourself so the real thing feels familiar.

Booking: use GOV.UK only

The theory test is booked on the official GOV.UK service. At the time of writing the fee is £23. Some unofficial websites add extra charges for the same booking, so always start from the official GOV.UK page. If you do not pass, you can rebook after a short wait, so do not be discouraged.

Once your theory test is behind you, you can focus on the road. Browse our driving courses or book your first lesson. Need a provisional licence first? See our provisional licence guide.
