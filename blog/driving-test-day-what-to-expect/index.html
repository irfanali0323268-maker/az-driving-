Practical Driving Test: What to Expect on the Day

By AZ Driving School · 12 November 2026 · 6 min read

Knowing what will happen on test day takes away a lot of the fear. Here is a plain guide to the UK car practical test, based on GOV.UK guidance. Always check the latest details on GOV.UK before your test.

Before you set off

Bring your photocard driving licence. The examiner will check your details and ask you to read a number plate from a distance of 20 metres (for a new-style plate). If you cannot read it, you will not be able to take the test.

Show me, tell me

You will be asked two vehicle safety questions. For the “tell me” question you explain how you would check something, such as the tyres or the brake lights. For the “show me” question you demonstrate it, usually while driving, for example how to clear the windscreen. A wrong answer is one minor fault, not an automatic fail, but your instructor can help you learn the answers well.

The driving

The test usually lasts about 40 minutes and includes general driving on a mix of roads. Expect:

  • Independent driving for around 20 minutes, where you follow directions from a sat nav or traffic signs.
  • One reversing manoeuvre, such as parallel parking at the roadside, parking in a bay, or pulling up on the right and reversing for two car lengths.
  • An emergency stop, which is asked on some tests.

You are allowed to ask the examiner to repeat or clarify an instruction. Take your time, and do not worry if you make a small mistake; carry on calmly.

How the test is marked

You can make up to 15 driving faults (minors) and still pass. You will fail if you make 16 or more, or if you make a serious or dangerous fault. Examiners are looking for safe, confident driving, not perfection.

How to prepare

  • Practise the roads and junction types used around your test centre with your instructor.
  • Do a mock test so the format feels familiar.
  • Get a good night’s sleep and arrive early.
  • Have a warm-up lesson before the test if you can.
